Privacy Policy

Cozy Brew: Coffee Timer · Effective 3 November 2026

The short version
  • Your brews, ratings, notes and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't collect, sell or share any personal information.

Information stored on your device

To work, Cozy Brew keeps the following on your iPhone only:

  • Your brew journal: the brews you finish, their ratings, and any beans and tasting notes you add.
  • Your settings, such as whether you use ounces or grams.
  • A brew in progress, so the timer can pick up where you left off if the app is closed.
  • How many free brews you've used.

This information isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or your computer, it may be included in those backups, which Apple's or your own backup settings control.

Subscriptions and payments

Cozy Brew Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and manages your subscription, including free trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds. The app only checks with Apple whether you have an active subscription. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's handling of this information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Cozy Brew uses them to tell you when the next brewing step starts and, if you start a free trial, to remind you a day before it ends. These notifications are scheduled on your iPhone; nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off anytime in your iPhone's Settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We don't sell or share information with third parties.

Children

Cozy Brew isn't directed at children, and because it doesn't collect personal information, it doesn't knowingly collect any from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding syncing between devices, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.
